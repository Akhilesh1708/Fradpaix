Expedition FAQ

All our expeditions above 6,000m require prior high-altitude trekking experience and strong cardiovascular fitness. We recommend 3–4 months of preparation including loaded hikes, stair climbs, and long-distance running. Prior 5,000m+ trekking experience is mandatory for most expeditions. Our team will guide you through a structured fitness plan after booking.

At altitudes above 5,000m, altitude sickness is a genuine risk. Every Fradpaix expedition is designed with mandatory acclimatisation days, load ferry rotations, and a "climb high, sleep low" approach. Our guides carry supplemental oxygen, pulse oximeters, and are trained in AMS recognition and evacuation procedures. Safety always comes before the summit.

Yes — all expeditions above 6,000m require prior crampon, ice axe, and fixed-rope experience. For some peaks (CB 13, CB 14, Shinkun West) we require prior 6,000m summit experience. Deo Tibba and Hanuman Tibba are suitable for strong trekkers transitioning to mountaineering. Our team can assess your experience during the registration process.

We provide all technical climbing equipment: ice axes, crampons, harnesses, helmets, jumars, descenders, fixed ropes, carabiners, and snow anchors. We also provide all camp equipment including tents, sleeping bags, mattresses, dining tent, and kitchen gear. You only need personal clothing, footwear, and accessories.
