About the Expedition

Mt. Yunam is a magnificent 6,111-meter peak located in Himachal Pradesh near Baralacha Pass in Lahaul Valley. This mountain offers beginner mountaineers an excellent introduction to high-altitude climbing through its non-technical approach and relatively straightforward summit route. The expedition typically begins from Manali and involves a systematic acclimatization process as teams move from base camp through multiple high camps before the final summit push.

The climb involves snow slopes, glacier travel, and basic mountaineering techniques including rope work, crampon use, and ice axe skills. From the summit, climbers are rewarded with breathtaking 360-degree views of the surrounding Himalayan giants including CB 13 (6,264m), CB 14 (6,070m), and the distant peaks of Zanskar and Ladakh ranges.

The best time to attempt Mt. Yunam is during the pre-monsoon season (May-June) and post-monsoon season (September-October) when weather conditions are most stable. The expedition requires participants to have good physical fitness, basic mountaineering knowledge, and the ability to cope with high-altitude conditions.

7-Day Detailed Itinerary

Today is the reporting day at Manali. After assembly and registration, drive to Batal through scenic landscapes. The journey takes you through Rohtang Pass and Kunzum Pass, offering spectacular mountain views throughout the day.

Overnight in camps at Batal. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Start the trek from Batal towards Mt. Yunam Base Camp via Baralacha Pass. The trail gradually ascends through stunning high-altitude terrain, crossing Baralacha La at 4,890m. Continue to Base Camp located at approximately 4,800m in a beautiful glacial valley.

Overnight in tents at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

This is a crucial acclimatization day at Base Camp. The team will conduct short acclimatization walks, practice rope techniques, crampon walking, and ice axe use. Our guides will provide essential mountaineering training and review safety protocols for the summit push.

Overnight in tents at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Trek from Base Camp to Summit Camp (also called Advanced Base Camp) at approximately 5,450 meters. The route involves glacier travel and snow slopes. This camp is strategically positioned for an early morning summit attempt. Team will prepare equipment and rest early for the summit push.

Overnight in high-altitude tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

The most important day of the expedition. Start extremely early at 2–3 AM from Summit Camp under a clear star-lit sky. The route ascends over steep snow slopes and mixed terrain with fixed ropes on technical sections. As altitude increases, every step demands focus and determination. As you approach the summit ridge, the views expand dramatically — a 360-degree panorama of CB 13 (6,264m), CB 14 (6,070m), the Spiti Valley, and the distant Zanskar ranges. After reaching the summit at 6,111m, descend carefully back to Base Camp.

Overnight in tents at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch + Dinner

This day is kept as a reserve buffer for the summit attempt in case of unfavourable weather on Day 5, or for additional rest and recovery after the summit. If the summit was successfully completed on Day 5, we use this day for a relaxed trek back toward Batal and onwards toward Manali.

Overnight as per itinerary progress. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Trek / drive back to Manali. The journey back is filled with memories of the summit, camaraderie, and the incredible landscapes of Lahaul. Arrive in Manali in the evening. The expedition officially concludes here with the distribution of summit certificates to all successful summiteers.

Trip ends at Manali. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch

Things to Carry

Climbing & Technical Gear

  • ID Card (Passport, visa, voter ID)
  • Down sleeping bag (rated to -20°C)
  • Down mattress (inflatable)
  • Waterproof stuff sack
  • Rucksack and duffle bag
  • Headlamp with extra batteries
  • Pocket Swiss knife
  • Trekking poles (adjustable)
  • Ice axe (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Crampons — 12-point (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Climbing harness & helmet (provided by Fradpaix)

Clothing & Personal Items

  • Down jacket or down pants
  • Hardshell waterproof jacket & trousers
  • Fleece jacket and track pants
  • Thermal base layers (2 sets)
  • Warm woolen socks (4–5 pairs)
  • Warm gloves (liner + waterproof shell)
  • Balaclava / neck gaiter
  • Sunglasses (UV400 / glacier glasses)
  • Gaiters (essential for snow travel)
  • Water bottles — 2 x 1L + thermos
  • Sunscreen (SPF 50+) and lip balm
  • Personal first aid kit & Diamox
  • Mountaineering boots (crampon-compatible)
