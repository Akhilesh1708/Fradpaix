About the Expedition

Standing at 5,289m amidst the 6,000m peaks of the Dhauladhar and Pir Panjal ranges, Friendship Peak acts as a breathtaking viewpoint surrounded by sky-scraping summits. On a clear day you can spot Mt. Hanuman Tibba, Shitidhar Peak, Mt. Deo Tibba, Indrasan, Inderkilla, Mt. Papsura, and countless unnamed peaks of the Dhauladhar range — a once-in-a-lifetime panorama.

Though a 5,000m peak with a shorter duration, Friendship Peak must not be underestimated. It tests skills and stamina at every turn. Prior high-altitude experience (at least 4,200–4,500m) is required along with snow trekking ability. The peak demands basic mountaineering knowledge — ice axe, harness, carabiners, crampons, descender, ice pitons — and rope technique on snow. Fradpaix guides train all participants thoroughly before and during the ascent.

7-Day Detailed Itinerary

All participants meet at Manali hotel. Rest after the journey. Our instructor briefs you on the upcoming expedition, clears all doubts, and checks all mandatory equipment. Overnight in hotel.

Overnight at hotel. Meal: Dinner

Drive to Dhundi hydro project after breakfast and begin trekking along the right bank of the Beas River. Trail passes through meadows, deodar ridge line, open grasslands, and wild strawberry patches before crossing a small river to reach Luhali Thach base camp. Acclimatisation walk after lunch. Dinner and overnight under a blanket of stars.

Overnight at Luhali Thach. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Trek through beautiful meadows with increasingly steep terrain, past a rocky patch to the base of the Lady Leg ridge. Reach the campsite with a magnificent view of Friendship Peak and surrounding 6,000m peaks. After lunch: equipment introduction, mountain terminologies, do's and don'ts, acclimatisation walk, and snow training.

Overnight at Lady Leg Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Leave for Summit Camp after breakfast through moraines and small streams. Trail leads to a large cairn on the ridge top — behind which is the camp. Establish tents, have an early dinner at 5–6 PM, sleep 5–6 hours. Wake up at 11:30 PM, gear up with all equipment, have a light meal, and start summit push by 12:00–12:30 AM.

Overnight at Summit Camp. Early dinner + midnight start. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Summit push begins at midnight. Initial moraines give way to snow. After 3–4 hours of traversal, reach the First Col at sunrise — spectacular views over Beas Valley with Hanuman Tibba glowing in morning light. Continue up the north ridge 100m, traverse left 300m, cross the first crevasse carefully, then ascend to the shoulder. Head left along the final ridge to the summit at 5,289m. Panoramic views of the Pir Panjal and Dhauladhar. Descend to Lady Leg Base Camp.

Overnight at Lady Leg Base Camp. Meal: Packed Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Reserved for a second summit attempt if Day 5 was unsuccessful due to weather or conditions. If summit was completed, use this as a rest day at base camp.

Used only if required. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Wake up for one last time in this magical mountain setting. Collect memories and descend to Dhundi via the same trail. Drive back to Manali — journey ends here. Note: itinerary may be altered due to unforeseen weather circumstances.

Departure day. Meal: Breakfast

Essential Things to Carry

Expedition & Technical Gear

  • Down sleeping bag (rated to -20°C)
  • Down mattress
  • Rucksack (min 70L) + duffle bag
  • Headlamp + 4 extra pairs of batteries (mandatory)
  • Trekking poles
  • Ice axe (provided by Fradpaix)
  • 12-point crampons (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Climbing harness & helmet (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Jumar / descender (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Waterproof stuff sack
  • Pocket Swiss knife
  • Power bank for phone and camera

Clothing & Personal

  • Wind & waterproof jacket (1) + Down jacket (1)
  • Fleece jacket (3) + Collared full-sleeve T-shirt (3)
  • Thermal / warmer — top and lower (1 set)
  • Trekking pants (2) + climbing pants (1) — both mandatory
  • Poncho / rain suit (mandatory)
  • Woolen cap (1), sun cap (1), neck warmer (1)
  • Woolen gloves (2) + waterproof gloves (1) + working gloves (1)
  • Warm socks (3) + woolen socks (2) + trek socks (2)
  • Waterproof trekking shoes — high ankle, good grip
  • UV-protected sunglasses (mandatory)
  • Water bottles 2×1L + thermos flask
  • Sunscreen SPF50+ and lip balm
  • Personal medicines + inhalers if applicable
  • Personal toilet kit, quick-dry towel, toilet paper
  • Original + photocopy of government ID
  • Medical certificate from registered practitioner (MBBS)
