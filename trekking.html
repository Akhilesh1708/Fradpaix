Trekking FAQ

Each trip is rated Easy, Moderate, or Challenging. For moderate treks, comfortable hiking 5–7 hours per day with a 6–8kg pack is sufficient. For challenging routes (EBC, Kilimanjaro), we recommend 3–4 months of cardiovascular training including stair climbs and loaded hikes.

On high-altitude treks, altitude sickness is a real consideration. Our itineraries are built with proper acclimatisation days built in. Our guides carry supplemental oxygen and pulse oximeters, and are trained to recognise AMS early. We can always descend if needed — your safety comes before the summit.

After booking you receive a detailed packing list tailored to your specific trek. In general: layers, a quality sleeping bag, sturdy boots, trekking poles, and a down jacket. We provide group shelter, cooking equipment, and a first-aid kit. Rental gear is available on request.

Yes — travel and evacuation insurance is mandatory for all Fradpaix expeditions. It must cover high-altitude trekking and medical helicopter evacuation. We recommend World Nomads or similar specialist providers. We're happy to advise on coverage requirements for each destination.

Absolutely — around 40% of our guests travel solo. We match you with a small, like-minded group of no more than 8 people. Solo supplements are available if you prefer your own tent. Many solo guests leave with lifelong friends.
