Kang Yatse I Expedition

Kang Yatse I is a technical peak and gives you a unique experience of climbing above the 6,000 m mountain peak in Ladakh. Being the tallest peak in the Hemis National Park, its parched terrain and demanding nature make Kang Yatse I peak climbing one of the most stimulating experiences in the mountains of India.

About the Expedition

OVERVIEW: Kang Yatse I, standing at an imposing 6,401 metres (21,001 ft), is one of the most coveted and technically demanding high-altitude peaks in the Ladakh region of the Indian Himalayas. Located in the Markha Valley within the Hemis National Park, this magnificent peak rises dramatically above the remote and starkly beautiful landscape of Ladakh, surrounded by a sea of glaciers, moraines, and ancient Buddhist wilderness that has remained largely untouched for centuries.

Unlike its neighbour Kang Yatse II (6,250 m), which is considered a trekking peak accessible to fit beginners with basic snow training, Kang Yatse I is a serious mountaineering objective that demands prior expedition experience, technical climbing skills, and a high level of physical fitness. The mountain presents climbers with a formidable combination of steep snow and ice faces, exposed ridgelines, and glaciated approaches that require confident use of crampons, ice axe, fixed ropes, and high-altitude camping equipment across multiple days above 5,000 metres.

The standard approach to Kang Yatse I begins from Leh, passing through the iconic villages of the Markha Valley — Chilling, Skiu, Markha, Hankar, and Nimaling — before reaching the base camp beneath the mountain's towering flanks at approximately 5,300 metres. The trek to base camp alone is a multi-day journey through one of Ladakh's most spectacular and remote valleys, offering dramatic views of Kang Yatse's twin summits, the Zanskar Range, and the high-altitude plateau of Nimaling.

17-Day Detailed Itinerary

Kang Yatse I expedition commences from Leh. Once you reach Leh, our representative will place you in a hotel pre-booked by Fradpaix Adventures. Explore the famous tourist spots in Leh at your convenience and spend the night at the hotel.

Overnight in the hotel in Leh.

This day is reserved for rest and acclimatization as you have gained significant altitude and your body needs to adjust accordingly. Enjoy local cuisine and culture, complete last-minute errands and attend the Fradpaix Adventures expedition briefing.

Overnight in the hotel in Leh.

After breakfast and check-out, head to Skiu. The drive from Leh to Skiu takes about three hours. After settling into the campsite and having lunch, take an acclimatization walk accompanied by the trek leaders and other climbers. Dinner and overnight stay at the campsite.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

Start early after breakfast for Markha Village. The trail passes through the dry terrain of Ladakh, sharp rocky peaks, poplar trees and muddy trails. Cross a small wooden bridge over a stream and continue through dirt roads and rocky riverbeds. On arrival, pitch tents with the help of the Fradpaix Adventures team.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

The terrain is comparatively easy, with long stretches of even ground and a few ascents and descents. Cross a river, reach Tacha Gompa and continue towards the Lower Hunkar campsite. Settle into camp amid impressive mountain views.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

Continue towards Nimaling through the high-altitude landscapes of the Markha Valley. The elevation gain requires a steady pace, hydration and careful acclimatization. Arrive at Nimaling and settle into camp for the night.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

After breakfast at the Nimaling camp, head towards Kang Yatse Base Camp. Climb through moraine and glacial terrain towards the mountain. The high-altitude environment marks the transition from trekking to expedition terrain. Overnight at base camp.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

This day is reserved for acclimatization to the thin air. The Fradpaix Adventures trek leader will conduct training on the proper use of climbing equipment including crampons, ice axes and snow boots. Participants will also practice ascending and descending while roped up. The second half of the day is for rest and recovery.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

After breakfast, pack the required equipment and undertake the load ferry to the Kang Yatse I Advance Base Camp. The route involves steep edges and loose moraine. The day prepares the team for the summit push. After dropping equipment and essentials, return to Kang Yatse Base Camp.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

Gain significant altitude while moving to the Kang Yatse I Advance Base Camp. The trail is tricky, but the previous day's load ferry provides familiarity with the terrain. On reaching the advance base camp, settle into the tent and rest.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

This day is reserved for acclimatization and final technical training. The Fradpaix Adventures trek leader will help participants practice using crampons and ascenders ahead of the summit attempt. Rest well and conserve energy for the summit push.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

Move from the Kang Yatse I Advance Base Camp to Camp 1 / Summit Camp. The climb involves steep and technical terrain and may take approximately five to six hours depending on conditions. Once at high camp, rest and prepare for the summit push.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

The summit push starts around midnight. Follow every instruction of the Fradpaix Adventures trek leader while climbing steep gradient slopes, ice walls and sharp ridges. The final section can involve technical climbing and vertical ice terrain. From the summit at 6,401 m, enjoy spectacular views of the surrounding Himalayan ranges before descending to Camp 1 for an overnight stay.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

Trace the route back to Kang Yatse Base Camp. After the demanding summit attempt, use the day to recover, hydrate and relax in the tents.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

This is a reserve day kept available in case unpredictable weather, route conditions or other mountain-related factors prevent the summit attempt on the planned summit day.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

After a hot breakfast, pack up the camp and begin the return journey. Traverse towards the grazing grounds of Nimaling via Kongmaru La Pass. Continue through the dramatic landscape before descending towards Chokdo/Chuskirmo and driving to Leh. Stay overnight at the pre-booked hotel.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner.

Check out from the hotel after breakfast. A Fradpaix Adventures representative will drive you to the airport for your onward journey. We hope to see you soon on your next adventure.

Meal: Breakfast.
