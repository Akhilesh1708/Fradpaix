About the Trek

The Miyar Valley trek is a remote, moderate-difficulty Himalayan trek in Lahaul & Spiti, Himachal Pradesh, often called "Himachal's Yosemite" or "Himachal's answer to Ladakh without the crowds." The valley stretches roughly 60 km point-to-point from Udaipur to the Kang La pass, passing through dramatic granite walls, high alpine pastures, and glacial rivers.

Starting from Manali, you drive through the Atal Tunnel and up the Chandrabhaga Valley to reach Udaipur, then follow the Miyar River to Khanjer — the last road-accessible village. From there the trail leads through lush farmlands, Buddhist chortens, boulder fields, and eventually to a hidden glacial lake near the snout of the Miyar Glacier.

With moderate daily distances and well-defined trails, this trek is ideal for beginners and those looking to experience true Himalayan wilderness away from the crowds. Peak wildflowers bloom in July–August.

7-Day Detailed Itinerary

Drive from Manali through the Atal Tunnel and up the Chandrabhaga Valley to reach Udaipur. Continue along the Miyar River to the picturesque village of Khanjer — the last road-accessible village, surrounded by lush fields and dramatic cliffs. Overnight in camps or a homestay.

Overnight at Khanjer. Meal: Dinner

Reserved for rest and acclimatization. Explore traditional homes, visit the village, or take short walks to nearby ridgelines. Use this day to get used to the altitude and prepare for the trail ahead.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

The trail begins gently through lush farmlands, Buddhist chortens, and flat alpine meadows. Expect to cross small rivulets. Camp at Yolling — a beautiful open site with views of the surrounding peaks.

Overnight in tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

A longer walk through boulder fields and high alpine pastures. The wide-open valley becomes narrower and more dramatic as granite walls close in. Zardong offers a majestic campsite surrounded by towering peaks on all sides.

Overnight in tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

A challenging day with multiple cold stream crossings — some deep, fast, and wide. Carry quick-drying sandals to protect your main trekking shoes. The trail winds through glacial outwash plains with dramatic views all around. Camp at Palpu, a remote site with a raw alpine feel.

Carry quick-drying sandals for stream crossings. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Hike to the hidden glacial lake near the snout of the Miyar Glacier. The lake's emerald-green waters surrounded by ice walls are the highlight of the entire trek. Later, descend gradually toward Gompa — a serene alpine campsite for the night.

Carry water for the full day — limited sources near the lake. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Retrace your steps to Khanjer through familiar meadows and campsites. With the hardest days behind you, this is a time for relaxed walks and quiet reflection. Drive back to Manali via Udaipur and the Atal Tunnel. Allow buffer time when booking onward travel.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch

Things to Carry

TREKKING GEAR & CLOTHING

  • Rucksack bag with rain cover
  • Headlamp with spare batteries
  • UV protection sunglasses
  • 2 water bottles (1 litre each)
  • High-ankle trekking shoes (non-skid)
  • Light sandals / quick-dry footwear
  • Quick-dry track pants (2 pairs)
  • Full-sleeve T-shirts / sweatshirts
  • Thermal body warmer (upper & lower)
  • Warm jacket (wrist & neck closed)
  • Rain wear — jacket & pants
  • Waterproof gloves, woolen cap, sun hat

DOCUMENTS & TOILETRIES

  • Original & photocopy of Govt. Photo ID (Aadhaar / Driving Licence / Passport)
  • Medical certificate from registered MBBS practitioner
  • Passport and visa for non-Indians
  • Personal toiletries kit (towel, soap, toothbrush)
  • Sunscreen lotion (SPF 50+) and lip balm
  • Personal medicines
  • Pocket Swiss knife
  • Trekking poles (adjustable)
