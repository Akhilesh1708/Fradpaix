About the Trek

The Ghepan Lake Trek is a mesmerizing 3-day journey to one of Lahaul Valley's most beautiful alpine lakes. Situated at 4,149 meters, Ghepan Lake is a pristine glacial lake surrounded by towering peaks and vast meadows, offering trekkers an unforgettable Himalayan experience.

Starting from the Lahaul region, this easy-level trek takes you through remote villages, lush green pastures, and dramatic mountain landscapes. The crystal-clear waters of Ghepan Lake reflect the surrounding snow-capped peaks, creating a breathtaking panorama that rewards every step of the journey.

With well-marked trails and moderate daily distances, this trek is suitable for beginners with basic fitness. The best time to visit is from June to September when the lake is free of ice and the meadows are carpeted with wildflowers.

3-Day Detailed Itinerary

Start from Keylong, the administrative headquarters of Lahaul. Drive to Jispa village, then begin trekking through beautiful meadows alongside the Bhaga River. The trail gradually ascends through pastures where shepherds graze their flocks in summer. Reach the base camp with views of surrounding peaks.

Water available at Jispa and along the river route. Overnight in tents.

The highlight day! Trek through alpine meadows carpeted with wildflowers in summer. The trail gradually climbs to reveal the stunning Ghepan Lake at 4,149m, surrounded by snow-capped peaks. Spend time at the lake enjoying the pristine beauty and mountain reflections. Return to base camp by evening.

Carry water for the full day — limited sources near the lake. Overnight in tents.

Descend back to Jispa village through the same scenic route. From Jispa, drive back to Keylong. You can explore Keylong town and visit nearby monasteries. End of trek with unforgettable memories of Ghepan Lake's serene beauty.

Water sources available along descent route.
