About the Expedition

Mt. CB 14 (Chandra Bhaga 14) is a 6,078–6,149-meter peak in Himachal Pradesh's Lahaul-Spiti region, often climbed alongside CB 13 as a technical duo in the Chandra Bhaga massif. It offers panoramic views of Chandratal Lake, Dhaka Glacier, and the Kunzum Range via boulder-strewn approaches from Batal, with expeditions spanning 10–17 days including acclimatisation hikes.

Situated near CB 13 on the South Dhaka Glacier, access starts from Manali through the Atal Tunnel to Batal base. Routes feature steep snow/ice slopes (50–70°), rocky pitches, and crevassed glaciers, rated moderate-to-difficult (Level 7), requiring crampons, ice axes, and prior 6,000m experience. Best windows are June–October, with early starts preferred for stable snow.

12-Day Detailed Itinerary

Arrive in Manali. Meet the Fradpaix team, complete documentation, equipment check, and receive a detailed briefing on the CB 14 expedition. Overnight in hotel.

Overnight at hotel in Manali. Meal: Dinner

Early morning drive from Manali to Batal via the Leh-Manali Highway passing Koksar, Chhatru, and Gramphu. Set up camp at Batal on arrival. Overnight at Batal campsite.

Overnight at Batal campsite. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

First load ferry day. Trek to base camp, drop equipment, and return to Batal for overnight stay. Acclimatisation day.

Overnight at Batal campsite. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Move permanently to Base Camp through rocky terrain with stream crossings. Fradpaix provides lunch en route. Rest overnight at Base Camp.

Overnight at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Load ferry to establish Camp 1. Route crosses high land, grassy slopes, boulders, and moraines. Return to Base Camp overnight.

Overnight at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Permanently move to Camp 1. Follow the same route as load ferry day. Overnight at Camp 1.

Overnight at Camp 1. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Push higher to Camp 2, navigating steeper glacier terrain and rocky sections. Acclimatise and rest. Overnight at Camp 2.

Overnight at Camp 2. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Ascend to Summit Camp. Enjoy panoramic views of snow-covered peaks. Rest and gather energy for the summit. Overnight at Summit Camp.

Overnight at Summit Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Begin summit push before dawn on fixed ropes through steep snow and technical sections. Summit CB 14 at 6,078m and enjoy views of Chandratal Lake, Dhaka Glacier, and surrounding peaks. Descend to Camp 1 overnight.

Overnight at Camp 1. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch + Dinner

Descend from Camp 1 to Base Camp. Rest and celebrate. Overnight at Base Camp.

Overnight at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Trek to Batal then drive to Manali arriving by evening. Overnight in Manali hotel.

Overnight at hotel in Manali. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch

Reserved for weather delays, additional summit attempts, or unforeseen circumstances.

Used only if required due to weather or itinerary changes.
