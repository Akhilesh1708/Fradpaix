About the Expedition

Shinkun South is a technically demanding Himalayan peak at approximately 6,127 meters near Shinkun La Pass in Himachal Pradesh. Expeditions start from Manali, driving to Jispa/Darcha, then trekking to Chuminakpo base camp at 4,450–4,650m near the pass. Routes involve southern flanks from Zanskar Sumdo, crossing glacial moraines and steeper ice faces toward the summit, with high camps at 5,100–5,500m.

The approach features challenging crevasses, 50-degree snow slopes, rock bands, and extreme weather — best tackled post-monsoon in September–October. Routes require crampons, ice axes, and prior 6,000m mountaineering experience. Fradpaix Adventures provides complete technical support including fixed ropes, certified guides, and all high-altitude equipment.

9-Day Detailed Itinerary

Meet at Nehru Park, Manali at 10 AM. Drive to Solang Valley. Equipment check and team introduction. Acclimatisation hike to Shagadugh after lunch. Return to Solang Valley campsite for dinner and overnight stay.

Overnight at Solang Valley campsite. Meal: Dinner

Drive through Atal Tunnel to Jispa via scenic snowy mountain ranges. Reach Jispa by noon, rest, and take an acclimatisation walk in the afternoon. Overnight at Jispa campsite.

Overnight at Jispa campsite. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Drive from Jispa to Shinkun South Base Camp on a challenging off-road route. Set up camp. Conduct training session on crampons, ice axes, ropes, and safety protocols. Overnight at Base Camp under clear mountain skies.

Overnight at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Load ferry carrying expedition equipment to Camp 1. Key acclimatisation day to familiarise with trail conditions and altitude. Return to Base Camp overnight.

Overnight at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Permanently move to Camp 1 carrying remaining equipment. Rest early for the summit push ahead. Overnight at Camp 1.

Overnight at Camp 1. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Ascend to Summit Camp via steep snow slopes and technical sections requiring fixed ropes and careful maneuvering. Set up camp, rest, and mentally prepare for the summit. Overnight at Summit Camp.

Overnight at Summit Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Summit push begins before dawn navigating steep inclines, icy ridges, and crevasses using crampons and ice axes. Standing atop Shinkun South at 6,127m, enjoy a 360° view of the Himalayan ranges — an unforgettable moment of triumph. Descend back to Camp 1 for the night.

Overnight at Camp 1. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch + Dinner

Reserved for summit re-attempt if Day 7 was unsuccessful due to weather or unforeseen circumstances.

Used only if required.

Trek from Camp 1 to Base Camp then drive via Jispa back to Manali by night. Final goodbyes to teammates. Expedition complete.

Overnight at hotel in Manali. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch
