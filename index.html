Who We Are

Built by Explorers,
For Explorers

Fradpaix brings you authentic Himalayan experiences with a focus on safety, sustainability, and genuine connection with the mountains. Based in the heart of Himachal Pradesh, we specialize in the challenging Bara Bhangal Trek.

We build every expedition with care: small groups, certified trek leaders, and complete support. Whether it's your first high-altitude trek or you're an experienced trekker, we're here to guide you through one of India's most spectacular mountain journeys.

  • Small Group Sizes

    Max 8 per expedition for a genuine backcountry feel.

  • Safety First

    Wilderness First Responder certified guides on every trip.

  • Leave No Trace

    We operate under strict LNT ethics and offset all carbon.
