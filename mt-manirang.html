About the Expedition

Mount Manirang is a formidable peak standing at around 6,593 meters, straddling the border between Spiti and Kinnaur districts in Himachal Pradesh, and ranks among the highest peaks in the region accessible for mountaineering expeditions. The Manirang Pass expedition is a high-altitude traverse across the border of the Kinnaur and Lahaul-Spiti districts, reaching an elevation of roughly 5,550–5,575 meters, following an ancient trade route right beside the mountain.

The approach typically begins from villages like Kaza or Mane in Spiti Valley, following the Manirang Nullah through rugged, high-altitude terrain before reaching base camp, from where climbers push toward the summit via glaciated slopes and technical sections requiring rope work, ice axes, and crampons.

Unlike the trekking routes in this region, Manirang is a genuine mountaineering objective rather than a trek, demanding prior climbing experience, proper technical gear, and a well-planned acclimatization schedule. The mountain offers sweeping views across the Spiti-Kinnaur watershed, with the Sutlej and Spiti river valleys visible from higher camps. The best climbing window falls between July and September.

Given its technical nature and remoteness, expeditions to Manirang are organized through experienced mountaineering outfits with proper permits, since the peak lies close to the Indo-Tibet border region and may have restricted area considerations.

19-Day Detailed Itinerary

Manali is a town right in the lap of the mountains and is one of India's most popular tourist destinations. Enjoy a fully guided tour of local markets and famous attractions by Fradpaix Adventures. You will also receive a detailed briefing on the plan of action for the expedition.

Overnight in hotel. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

After breakfast, check out of the hotel and drive from Manali to Kaza via the famous Atal Tunnel and Kunzum Pass (4,551 m) — the highest pass of the route. Fully off-road experience with views of Shikhar Bey, Mount CB 13, and the striking Himachali landscapes all the way to Kaza.

Overnight in Kaza. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Full day rest at Kaza for acclimatization. The Fradpaix team handles documentation with local administration and arranges loads for porters/mules in the evening.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

After breakfast, drive to Mane Village. The distinctive Spitian architecture and rugged Himalayan beauty will leave a lasting impression. Overnight stay arranged by Fradpaix in a homestay or campsite.

Overnight at Mane Village. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Set off early around 5–6 AM from Mane Village. The trail follows a gradual ascent through rocky terrain and barren Spiti-style landscapes. Cross the initial ridge, reach Sopona Lake and pass through its dry lake bed. The climb steepens with loose scree and gravel. The Intermediate Camp at 4,700m offers a panoramic view of Manirang Peak surrounded by glaciers.

Overnight in Fradpaix tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

After breakfast, start trekking to Base Camp (5,100 m). Begin around 10 AM — the trail involves a steady, steep ascent with loose scree and patches of snow. The air thins significantly. After 5–6 hours, reach Base Camp on a rocky plateau with sweeping views of snow-capped peaks and glaciers.

Overnight in Fradpaix tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Full day rest at Base Camp for acclimatization. Allow your body to adjust to the extreme altitude before ascending higher. Light walks around the camp are beneficial.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

The guest team and guide team trek towards Camp 01 to ferry equipment loads. Packed lunch provided. Return to Base Camp in the evening for overnight stay.

Overnight in tents. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch + Dinner

Complete rest day at Base Camp. Conserve energy and stay hydrated in preparation for the high-altitude push ahead.

Overnight in tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Start early around 8 AM. The route involves steep ascents over rocky ridges, snowfields, and occasional ice patches. After steady climbing, reach Camp 1 on a narrow ridge or snow plateau with breathtaking views of surrounding glaciers and the Manirang summit ridge. This camp is crucial for acclimatization before the summit push.

Overnight in high-altitude tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

The team climbs towards Camp 02 to open the route and fix ropes on technical sections. Return to Camp 01 in the evening.

Overnight in high-altitude tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Starting around 10 AM, ascend the challenging mixed-terrain route — rocky sections, loose scree, high winds and thin air. Camp 02 is perched on a rocky or narrow ridge offering spectacular views of the summit route and surrounding Himalayan peaks. Focus on setting up tents, resting, and preparing for the summit push.

Overnight in high-altitude tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

After breakfast, ascend towards the summit camp. Most teams directly climb Manirang from Camp 02, depending on weather and team speed. Establish the summit camp in the safest available location.

Overnight in high-altitude tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

The most important day. Begin early, climbing over rock and ice slopes on previously fixed ropes, ascending snow slopes to reach the most demanding section. The final stretch involves a narrow steep ridge — focus and precision are crucial. From the summit, witness a bird's-eye view of Reo & Leo Pargail, Mount Gang Chua, Mt Ibex, Granite Peak, and countless peaks above 6,000 m blanketed in shimmering white snow. Descend to Camp 01 or Camp 02 after the summit.

Overnight in high-altitude tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Reserve day for the summit push in case of unfavourable weather or team readiness on Day 14.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

One additional buffer day reserved for the summit push if any unforeseeable changes occur — weather windows, acclimatization issues, or team pace.

Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Return to Base Camp to celebrate the safety and success of the expedition with all team members. In the evening, clean the entire Base Camp in preparation for early morning departure towards Mane Village.

Overnight in tents. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Trek from Base Camp to Mane Village after breakfast. Overnight stay at Mane Village.

Overnight at Mane Village. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

After breakfast, drive back to Manali. The journey marks the end of an extraordinary mountaineering expedition. Depart with the memories of standing on one of Himachal's highest summits.
