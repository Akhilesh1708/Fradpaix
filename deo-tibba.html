About the Expedition

Deo Tibba (6,001m) is a striking peak in the Pir Panjal Range, located southwest of Manali above the village of Jagatsukh in Kullu District. The name means "Hills of the Gods" — the dome-shaped summit is believed to be a gathering place for Devtas, giving it deep religious significance. The approach passes through dense Oak and deodar forests, wildflower meadows, and alpine lakes, making this one of the most scenic expedition routes in Himachal Pradesh.

The expedition is best suited for climbers with prior high-altitude experience and good physical fitness. The technical sections include steep snow and glacier terrain, a challenging moraine approach, and load ferries to establish camps at altitude. The best climbing windows are May–October, with Fradpaix Adventures providing complete support — from IMF permits and certified guides to technical equipment and high-altitude camps.

12-Day Detailed Itinerary

Drive from Manali to Khanol then trek to Chikka through alpine forests of Oak and deodar. The trail passes Sethan — a famous rock climbing and snowboarding site — before reaching Chikka with stunning views of Deo Tibba, Spiti Valley, and Indrasan Peak.

Overnight at Chikka campsite. Meal: Dinner

Trek through dense forest that transitions to scattered trees and alpine meadows along Jagatsukh Nallah. Careful navigation over boulders and slippery rocks. Reach the Panduropa camp at Seri with views of Norbu cliffs, Malana Pass, and parts of Deo Tibba. Overnight with bonfire at campsite.

Overnight at Seri campsite. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Challenging day following the Jagatsukh river past a waterfall and monument. Steep cliff section for 2 hours followed by moraines. Reach Tainta Camp (Base Camp) and move up to visit Chota Chandratal Lake (2 hrs hike) surrounded by wildflowers and Deo Tibba summit towers. Return to Base Camp for overnight.

Overnight at Base Camp (Tainta). Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Full acclimatisation day at Base Camp. The body adjusts to altitude. Optional short hike to Chota Chandratal. Technical gear briefing and ice/snow skills session in the evening. Rest well for the load ferry ahead.

Overnight at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Load ferry carrying vital equipment to Camp 1 at the Dhanuan Pass. 200m moderate climb, then 300m steep climb, followed by 100m flat approach. Snow-covered slope — walk carefully with crampons. Packed lunch en route. Return to Base Camp overnight.

Overnight at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch + Dinner

Permanently move to Camp 1 following the same route as the load ferry day. Walk carefully with guide on rocky snow terrain. Deep crevasse sections require caution. Overnight at Camp 1 in high-altitude tents.

Overnight at Camp 1. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Acclimatisation rotation to Summit Camp carrying minimal gear. Snow terrain with moderate to challenging climbing. Spend time at Summit Camp to gauge conditions, then return to Camp 1 overnight to avoid oxygen issues at extreme altitude.

Overnight at Camp 1. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch + Dinner

Permanently move to Summit Camp carrying only essential items. Ice and snow skills practice on the approach. Rest early and sleep by 8 PM — the summit push begins before dawn tomorrow. Overnight at Summit Camp.

Overnight at Summit Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Early morning summit push starting before dawn. Steep initial climb on snow followed by technical sections with deep cracks — walk carefully. A flat ridge leads to the wide summit plateau. Witness panoramic views of Indrasan Peak, surrounding glaciers, and the vast Himalayan horizon. Descend to Camp 1 due to oxygen levels at altitude.

Overnight at Camp 1. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch + Dinner

Return journey begins. Careful descent on steep terrain — keep knees slightly bent. Follow the same path used on ascent. Aim to reach by lunchtime. Rest and celebrate the summit. Overnight at Base Camp.

Overnight at Base Camp. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Long descent via mixed steep and flat trail from Base Camp all the way to Chikka. Last tent stay of the expedition — enjoy it to the fullest. Carry packed lunch for the journey. Overnight at Chikka campsite.

Overnight at Chikka campsite. Meal: Breakfast + Packed Lunch + Dinner

Final trek to Khanol then drive back to Manali. Walk carefully to avoid injury on the descent. Collect all equipment and say goodbye to the hills. Receive your summit certificate from Fradpaix Adventures. Expedition complete.

Departure day. Meal: Breakfast

Essential Things to Carry

Expedition & Technical Gear

  • Down sleeping bag (rated to -20°C)
  • Down mattress (inflatable)
  • Rucksack (min 70L) + duffle bag
  • Headlamp + 4 extra pairs of batteries
  • Trekking poles (adjustable)
  • Ice axe (provided by Fradpaix)
  • 12-point crampons (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Climbing harness & helmet (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Jumar / descender (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Waterproof stuff sack
  • Pocket Swiss knife
  • Power bank for phone and camera

Clothing & Personal

  • Down jacket
  • Hardshell waterproof jacket & trousers
  • Fleece jacket & thermal base layers (2 sets)
  • Trekking pants (2) and climbing pants (1)
  • Woolen socks (4–5 pairs)
  • Warm gloves — liner + waterproof shell
  • Balaclava / neck gaiter
  • Poncho / rain suit (mandatory)
  • Glacier sunglasses (UV400)
  • Gaiters (essential for snow)
  • Mountaineering boots (crampon-compatible)
  • Water bottles 2×1L + thermos flask
  • Sunscreen SPF50+ and lip balm
  • Personal first aid kit & Diamox
  • Original + photocopy of government ID
  • Medical certificate from registered practitioner
