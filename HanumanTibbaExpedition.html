About the Expedition

Mt. Hanuman Tibba at 5,982m is one of the most thrilling peaks to summit in the Pir Panjal Range of the Himalayas near Manali. The trail to this pyramid shaped peak passes through the beautiful alpine meadows of Solang Valley, glaciers and rivers of Beas Kund and finally across the imposing Tentu Pass giving you stunning and diverse change in scenery all throughout the trek. This expedition is meant for those aspiring to push their limits and take it up a notch from regular trekking but only with prior trekking experience since the Hanuman Tibba expedition is no small feat involving technical climb and high fitness level to successfully undertake this epic expedition.

Prior technical knowledge of the use of crampons and jumar is a must. The summit offers you unimaginable views of the ocean of mountains that you get to witness thus making this expedition a once in a lifetime opportunity. The best time to do the expedition is between the months of June and October.

12-Day Detailed Itinerary

You have to reach Manali by 11 am along with the trek members. The team will assemble, complete the required permits and documents, meet all participants, discuss the schedule and stay overnight at a hotel in Manali.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

After breakfast, drive from Manali to Dhundi and trek towards Bakkarthatch and Beas Kund Base Camp. The trail follows the Beas River, crosses a wooden bridge and continues through lush green trails towards the glacier and campsite.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

A complete rest and training day at Base Camp. The team will conduct a short acclimatization hike, crampon fitting, technical gear distribution and a general briefing on the upcoming expedition.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

The porter team will carry loads while the instructors conduct full-day glacier training. Participants learn ice-climbing techniques, use of climbing equipment and teamwork procedures required for the expedition.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Early morning climb towards Camp 1 through steep snow slopes and the Tentu Pass. The team will carry loads, pitch camp and work together on the glacier. From this point the expedition camps are established on snow and glacier terrain.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Early morning climb towards Camp 2 over snowy glacier slopes near the glacier lake. The route is steep and demanding and requires careful movement over snow and ice.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

A challenging day towards Summit Camp. The team will assess weather, snowfall and glacier conditions and fix ropes where required. The route includes rocky and glacier terrain with steep sections.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Summit attempt on Hanuman Tibba. The climb involves steep snow and glacier terrain, crevasses and technical sections. After reaching the summit, the team descends back to Summit Camp.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Descend from Summit Camp to Camp 1 through rocky, glacier and steep descent sections. This day also serves as a reserve day if the first summit attempt is cancelled due to weather.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Early morning descent from Camp 1 towards Base Camp. The trail can be steep or slippery in places, after which the team returns to Base Camp for rest and recovery.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

After breakfast, trek back to Dhundi by the same route and drive to Manali. Celebrate the successful expedition with the team and stay overnight at a hotel in Manali.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

This day is kept as a buffer for the Hanuman Tibba Expedition and will be used if required due to weather, summit conditions or itinerary changes.

Overnight as per itinerary. Meal: Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner

Essential Things to Carry

Expedition & Technical Gear

  • ID Card (Passport, visa, voter ID)
  • Down sleeping bag (rated to -20°C)
  • Down mattress (inflatable)
  • Waterproof stuff sack
  • Rucksack and duffle bag
  • Headlamp with extra batteries
  • Pocket Swiss knife
  • Trekking poles (adjustable)
  • Ice axe (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Crampons — 12-point (provided by Fradpaix)
  • Climbing harness & helmet (provided by Fradpaix)

Clothing & Personal Items

  • Down jacket or down pants
  • Hardshell waterproof jacket & trousers
  • Fleece jacket and track pants
  • Thermal base layers (2 sets)
  • Warm woolen socks (4–5 pairs)
  • Warm gloves (liner + waterproof shell)
  • Balaclava / neck gaiter
  • Sunglasses (UV400 / glacier glasses)
  • Gaiters (essential for snow travel)
  • Water bottles — 2 x 1L + thermos
  • Sunscreen (SPF 50+) and lip balm
  • Personal first aid kit & Diamox
  • Mountaineering boots (crampon-compatible)
Expedition Equipment Note

Fradpaix provides all technical climbing gear — ice axes, crampons, harnesses, helmets, jumars, and fixed ropes. You only need personal clothing and accessories. A full gear list is shared after booking.
